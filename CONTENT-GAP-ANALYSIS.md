## 2. Blog & Content Gap Clusters

Currently, the blog has minimal posts. Recommended 9 core clusters:
1. **Armenian Traditional Cuisine:** History & craft of *Ishli Kufta*, *Pasuts Dolma*, *Tatar Boraki*, *Khash*.
2. **Homemade Frozen Food:** How shock-freezing preserves taste without preservatives or soy.
3. **How to Cook Frozen Products:** Step-by-step boiling, pan-searing, oven, and Air Fryer guides.
4. **Quick Family Meals:** 10-minute weeknight dinners & kid-friendly colorful pelmeni ideas.
5. **Healthy Eating:** Balanced protein-rich meals (all-beef kololak, chicken breast nuggets, spinach vareniki).
6. **Vegan & Fasting Food:** Complete Armenian *Մեծ Պահք* (Great Lent) & vegan meal planning.
7. **Cooking Tips:** Why you shouldn't thaw khinkali/pelmeni before boiling + homemade sauces (matzoon-garlic, tahini).
8. **Product Comparisons:** *Siberian vs. Signature Pelmeni*; *Pan-fried vs. Air Fryer Cutlets & Falafel*.
9. **Storage & Preparation Instructions:** Safe `-18°C` freezer storage & insulated delivery best practices.
