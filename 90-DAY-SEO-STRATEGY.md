## 2. Local SEO — Armenia & Yerevan

- **NAP Consistency:** Standardize across Footer, Contact page, Google Business Profile, and `FoodEstablishment` JSON-LD:
  - **Name:** `Gayane's Kitchen | Գայանեի Խոհանոց`
  - **Address:** `ք. Երևան, 0054, Դավթաշեն 2-րդ թղմ. 37` (Davtashen 2nd dist. 37, Yerevan 0054, Armenia)
  - **Phones:** `+374 55 600655`, `+374 11 600111` (note: check `gv2_phone` Customizer setting so Schema doesn't fall back to placeholder `+374 99 00 00 00`!)
  - **Delivery Consistency:** Confirm whether free delivery threshold is `10,000 AMD` or `15,000 AMD` (top bar in screenshot showed `10,000 ֏` while FAQ JSON-LD mentioned `15,000 ֏`) and unify everywhere!
- **Google Business Profile (GBP):** Set primary category *Frozen food store* / *Armenian restaurant* / *Food producer*, add product catalog items, weekly posts linking to `/shop/`, and encourage verified customer reviews.
- **Local Citations:** Spyur.am, Yell.am, Google Maps, Yandex Maps, 2GIS Yerevan.

## 3. Conversion-Oriented SEO Priorities

1. **Unify Free Delivery Messaging:** Ensure the header promo bar, cart progress bar, and FAQ schema display the exact same WooCommerce shipping threshold.
2. **Product Photos:** Replace placeholder images on imported products with studio photography — visual trust directly drives add-to-cart rate on food e-commerce.
3. **Clear Cooking Time & Portion Badges:** Highlight *"Ready in 6–12 min"* and *"100% Natural / No Soy"* near the Add button on mobile.
